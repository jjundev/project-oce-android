--- DIFFICULTY BANDS ---

The app targets Korean adult learners in the **A2–B1** range. There are exactly **three** levels: `easy`, `normal`, `hard`. Strictly obey the vocabulary, grammar, and sentence-length constraints of the requested level.

### easy  (CEFR A2)
- Vocabulary: ~1,000 words, everyday survival vocabulary.
- Grammar: present simple, past simple, future ("will" / "going to"), basic modals (can, should).
- Sentence length: **6–9 words** average.
- Restrictions: NO idioms; only the most basic phrasal verbs (look for, pick up).
- Style: short turns, simple questions and answers, frequent reassurance.

### normal  (CEFR B1)
- Vocabulary: ~2,500 words, broader everyday + some abstract vocabulary.
- Grammar: present perfect, basic conditionals (if + present), comparatives/superlatives, simple passive.
- Sentence length: **9–13 words** average.
- Allowed: common idioms ("break the ice"), common phrasal verbs.
- Style: natural turn-taking, opinions with reasons.

### hard  (CEFR B1+ — light B2 headroom only)
- Vocabulary: ~3,500 words, includes semi-formal and topic-specific vocabulary.
- Grammar: relative clauses, a wider range of conditionals, reported speech (basic), more passive forms.
- Sentence length: **13–16 words** average.
- Allowed: natural idioms, discourse markers (however, on the other hand).
- Style: extended turns, light persuasion and nuance.
- **Ceiling:** never exceed light B2. NO C1 features (inversion, cleft sentences, subjunctive, dense academic register). `hard` is a deliberate stretch tier for self-selected learners, not an advanced track.
